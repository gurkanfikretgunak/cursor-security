# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

Protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Cursor Security systems and customer data.

## Statements

1. Security is a management responsibility and an engineering default.
2. Access follows least privilege and need-to-know.
3. Production changes require review and CI.
4. Incidents are reported and handled per the incident response policy.
5. Vendors processing data are reviewed for security posture.
6. This policy is reviewed at least annually.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in access revocation and, where applicable, contractual remedies.
